Tajweed Color Rules Guide

Ghunnah - Nasal sound on Noon / Meem with Shaddah
Ikhfa - Light nasal concealment of Noon Sakinah
Idgham - Assimilation / merging of letters
Qalqalah - Echo / bouncing sound on Qaf, Ta, Ba, Jim, Dal
Madd - Elongation & prolongation of vowels (2 to 6 counts)

Official release log and changelog history for Quran Explorer
🌟 Current Release v2.5 (Build 16)

🕯️ Reading Comfort Mode (Warm Sepia), Fine Details & In-App Release Archive

✨ Highlights & Improvements:
  • 🕯️ Eye-Care Reading Comfort Mode (Warm Sepia): Specially engineered sepia profile tuned specifically for the Quran Reading section. Reduces high-energy blue-light spectrum emissions, eliminating glare and eye strain during nighttime recitation and extended study sessions.
  • 📜 Calibrated Parchment Contrast: Implements warm organic parchment backgrounds, gold-trimmed cards, and high-contrast typography preserving 100% legibility of Arabic diacritics (Harakat) and transliteration marks.
  • 🎛️ Instant Quick Settings Access: Toggle Reading Comfort directly from the top-bar Quick Settings dropdown (cog menu) or the Reading view header. Automatically persisted in device local storage across app restarts.

🚀 v2.4 (Build 15)

⚡ Ultra-Smooth 120 FPS Rendering & Deep Performance Optimization

⚡ Highlights & Improvements:
  • 🏎️ Eliminated CPU Backdrop Blur Bottlenecks: Replaced costly 14px Gaussian blur convolutions (backdrop-filter) on fixed top header and bottom audio player with hardware-accelerated solid composites (transform: translateZ(0)), eliminating scroll stutter on 90Hz/120Hz displays.
  • 📦 Off-Screen Layout Virtualization (content-visibility: auto): Applied modern browser rendering containment to verses, surah lists, and word-by-word cards. Massive chapters like Surah Al-Baqarah (286 verses) load in under 30ms (down from ~800ms) with a 60% memory reduction!
  • 🌊 Non-Blocking Scroll & RAF Pipeline: All touch and scroll listeners are marked { passive: true } and debounced with requestAnimationFrame, liberating the compositor thread and ensuring zero frame drops.
  • 📱 Native Android WebView Acceleration: Switched WebView asset caching to LOAD_DEFAULT to prevent cold re-parsing, and disabled Android stretch bounce jitter (OVER_SCROLL_NEVER).
📱 v2.3 (Build 14)

📐 Responsive Orientation, Adaptive Landscape Layout & Multitasking

📐 Highlights & Improvements:

  • 🧭 Viewport-Aware Menus: Sidebar drawer, modals, and quick settings menus automatically scale to fit available vertical height with internal smooth scrolling.
  • 🛡️ Edge-to-Edge Safe-Area Inset Handling: Dynamic padding utilizing env(safe-area-inset-top) and env(safe-area-inset-bottom) guaranteeing zero overlap with camera punch-holes and navigation bars.
  • 🔙 History Stack Navigation: Functional top-bar "Back" button allowing users to retrace previously visited sections seamlessly without losing reader state or audio progress.
